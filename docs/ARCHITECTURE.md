## Assemblies
Assembly definitions (`.asmdef`) are Unity's equivalent of separate `.csproj` projects.

| Assembly | Folder | References | Platforms |
|---|---|---|---|
| `WoodcraftSimulator.Domain` | `Scripts/Domain/` | none (`noEngineReferences: true`) | all |
| `WoodcraftSimulator` | `Scripts/` | `Unity.InputSystem`, `WoodcraftSimulator.Domain` | all |
| `WoodcraftSimulator.Editor` | `Scripts/Editor/` | `WoodcraftSimulator`, `WoodcraftSimulator.Domain` | Editor only |
| `WoodcraftSimulator.Tests.EditMode` | `Tests/EditMode/` | `WoodcraftSimulator.Domain`, NUnit, Test Runner | Editor only, `UNITY_INCLUDE_TESTS` |

A nested `.asmdef` claims its folder, so `Scripts/Domain/` and `Scripts/Editor/` are excluded from the runtime assembly.
Domain types and their Unity counterparts share namespaces across assemblies (e.g. `WoodcraftSimulator.Materials`).

Namespaces mirror folders: `WoodcraftSimulator.Player`, `WoodcraftSimulator.Editor`, ...

## Layering
- **Domain (plain C#):** measurement, timber dimensions, cutting, BOM, inventory rules, job validation.
  Lives in `WoodcraftSimulator.Domain`, which cannot reference `UnityEngine` (enforced by the compiler)
  → EditMode unit tests, also runnable with `dotnet test tools/DomainTests`.
- **Presentation (MonoBehaviour):** scene objects, input, rendering, physics; reads/writes domain state.
- **Definitions (ScriptableObject):** static configuration (wood species, fasteners, tools, jobs). Never used as save data.

## Units
- World: 1 Unity unit = 1 meter.
- Domain lengths: `Length`, integer tenths of a millimeter (0.1 mm resolution, see D-014). Converted to
  meters (float) only for Unity world space.

## Systems

### Player (`WoodcraftSimulator.Player`)
`PlayerController`: `CharacterController`-based first-person movement and mouse look.
- Origin at the feet; child camera at 1.65 m eye height. Yaw rotates the body, pitch rotates the camera.
- Input from project-wide actions: `Player/Move`, `Player/Look`, `Player/Sprint`, `Player/Jump`.
- Look sensitivity comes from `UserPreferences` (settable at runtime via `LookSensitivity`).
- Knows nothing about pausing or the cursor: `PauseController` disables it while paused.
- Precision look: holding `Player/PrecisionLook` (RMB) narrows the FOV to 25° and scales look sensitivity by
  FOV / normal FOV (D-025).

### Core (`WoodcraftSimulator.Core`)
- `PauseController` (own root object `PauseController`): single owner of paused state + cursor lock.
  `Player/Pause` (Esc / gamepad Start) toggles; window focus loss pauses. Paused = `Time.timeScale 0`,
  cursor free, every Behaviour in `disableWhilePaused` disabled. Raises `PausedChanged(bool)`.
- `UserPreferences`: static, stateless wrapper over `PlayerPrefs` for settings (mouse sensitivity). Not for save data.
- `PhysicsQueries`: ray/sphere casts (NonAlloc) that skip one hierarchy, used for all casts from the player's eyes (D-023).
- `BoxMeshBuilder`: exact-size box mesh, UVs in meters.

### Interaction (`WoodcraftSimulator.Interaction`)
- `IInteractable`: `InteractionPrompt` + `Interact(PlayerInteractor)`. Any component on the hit collider's
  GameObject or a parent. New interactable types = new components; no central switch statement.
- `PlayerInteractor` (on Player): raycast from the camera each frame (2.5 m reach, triggers ignored, player skipped, D-023), then a
  0.08 m SphereCast aim assist if the ray missed every interactable (first contact only),
  forwards `Player/Interact` (E). `GetComponentInParent<IInteractable>` per cast, no allocations.
  Raises `TargetChanged(IInteractable)` on focus change and after each interaction → UI is event-driven.
- Disabled while paused (via `PauseController`); `OnDisable` clears focus so the prompt hides.
- `IsBlocked`: suspends interactions without touching `enabled` (used while carrying; independent of pause).
- `IDescribable`: optional `Description` used by generic components to build prompts.

### Handling (`WoodcraftSimulator.Handling`)
- `Carryable` (`IInteractable`, needs Rigidbody + BoxCollider): prompt "Pick up <description>"; on E asks the
  player's `PlayerCarrier` to pick it up. `SetCarried` switches colliders off/on; kinematic in both states.
  The BoxCollider is the placement footprint.
- `PlayerCarrier` (on Player, disabled while paused): carries one object at a time at `holdOffset` (yaw-only),
  R/T rotate, LateUpdate computes the placement pose and validity (raycast + `Physics.OverlapBoxNonAlloc`, D-019),
  exposes `Status`/`Blocker` + `PlacementStatusChanged` (D-022, HUD shows why placement is not possible),
  shows a translucent preview (`PlacementPreview` material, MaterialPropertyBlock green/red), LMB places.
  Raises `CarryingChanged(bool)`; sets `PlayerInteractor.IsBlocked` while carrying.

### UI (`WoodcraftSimulator.UI`)
`GameplayHud` (on `HUD` with `UIDocument`): queries `crosshair` / `interaction-prompt` from `Hud.uxml`,
subscribes to `PlayerInteractor.TargetChanged` and `PlayerCarrier.CarryingChanged`, toggles USS classes
(`crosshair--focused`, `hidden`). While carrying, the prompt shows the carry controls instead.
The key label comes from the action binding (`GetBindingDisplayString`), so rebinding updates the prompt.

`PauseMenu` (on `PauseMenu` with `UIDocument`, `sortingOrder` 10 → above the HUD; same PanelSettings/panel):
shown/hidden on `PausedChanged`; Resume, mouse-sensitivity slider (applies live to `PlayerController` and
stores in `UserPreferences`, saved when the menu closes), Quit (exits Play Mode in the Editor).

### Measurement (`WoodcraftSimulator.Measurement`, Domain)
`Length`: readonly struct, `long` count of 0.1 mm. Exact `+ - *`, comparisons, conversions to mm/cm/m,
invariant formatting ("2400 mm", "2.5 mm"). Doubles are rounded to the nearest 0.1 mm on the way in.
`RoundTo(step)`, `Min/Max/Clamp`.
`TapeMeasure` (static): `Read(pieceLength, pointFromStart, hookedEnd, resolution)`, `DistanceFromHook`, `NearestEnd`;
`BoardEnd` Start (local −Z) / End (local +Z).

### Materials
Domain (`WoodcraftSimulator.Domain`):
- `BoardDimensions`: actual length × width × thickness (`Length`), positive; volume in m³.
- `WoodSpecies`: id, display name, density (kg/m³). Immutable.
- `TimberPiece`: species + dimensions, mass = volume × density. Immutable (cutting will create new pieces).

Unity side (`WoodcraftSimulator`):
- `WoodSpeciesDefinition` (ScriptableObject): editable species data incl. greybox color and data notes; `ToDomain()`.
  Seven assets in `ScriptableObjects/WoodSpecies/` (spruce, pine, oak, beech, walnut, larch, Douglas fir) with
  **approximate** densities flagged for verification (D-017).
- `TimberBoard` (MonoBehaviour): serialized species + mm dimensions → `TimberPiece` (source of truth) →
  generated box mesh (`BoxMeshBuilder`, UVs in meters), `BoxCollider` size, `Rigidbody` mass, color via
  `MaterialPropertyBlock` on the shared `Lumber` material. Transform scale stays 1. Axes: length Z, width X, thickness Y.
  Implements `IDescribable` ("Spruce 2400 × 95 × 45 mm (4.6 kg)"). Boards are kinematic + `Carryable` (D-019).

### Inventory (`WoodcraftSimulator.Inventory`)
Domain (`WoodcraftSimulator.Domain`):
- `InventoryItem`: one physical item instance (identity by reference), `DefinitionId`, `DisplayName`, `ItemSizeClass` (Small/Large).
- `ToolBelt`: fixed slots for **small** items; `TryAdd` → Added/Full/TooLarge/AlreadyStored; `Select(slot)` takes an
  item in hand, selecting the same or an empty slot holsters; `TryRemove`; `Changed` event only on real changes.

Unity side:
- `ItemDefinition` (ScriptableObject): id, display name, size class; `CreateInstance()`.
- `PickupItem` (`IInteractable`, `IDescribable`): world object for a small item; "Take <name>" → `PlayerToolBelt.TryStore`.
  Holds its hand pose (relative to the camera). Colliders off while stored.
- `PlayerToolBelt` (on Player, disabled while paused): wraps the domain `ToolBelt` (4 slots), keys `Player/ToolSlot1..4`,
  shows only the selected item (in hand, follows the camera in LateUpdate). A newly taken item goes into the hand (D-027).
  Hands-full rule: picking up a `Carryable` holsters the tool, putting it down takes it back; slot keys are ignored
  while carrying. Raises `Changed`.
- HUD: belt bar at the bottom (`belt` container, slots built in `GameplayHud.Start` from the slot count).
- No way to put a small item back into the world yet.
- Equips the `IHeldTool` of the item in hand (`HeldTool`), unequips on holster and while paused (OnDisable).

### Tools (`WoodcraftSimulator.Tools`)
- `IHeldTool` + `HeldToolContext` (eyes, player root, belt): shared contract for tools in hand (D-025).
- `BoardAim` (serializable plain class, used by both tools): raycast (player skipped) onto a `TimberBoard`, hook on the
  nearest end, sticky hook (D-026), returns a `BoardAimPoint` (board, local Z, hooked end, face turned to the player).
  `TapeBladeView` draws the blade LineRenderer on that face.
- `TapeMeasureTool`: `TapeMeasure.Read` at the aimed point, `Readout` "623 mm   (board 2400 mm)".
- `PencilTool` (D-028): needs a tape measure on the belt; live reading + target; `Player/UseTool` (LMB/RT) adds a
  domain `PencilMark` (exact position, reference end) via `TimberBoard.AddMark` and reports `Tolerance.Check` against
  the `CutList` target. `CutList`: note on the bench, E cycles preset targets (300/450/600/900/1200 mm).
- `TimberBoard.Marks` (`BoardMarks`) + mark visuals under a `PencilMarks` child (shared unit cube mesh, `PencilMark`
  material). `TimberBoard.SetPiece(piece, marks)` re-targets a board (used for the halves of a cut).
- `IPlacementTarget` (D-033): `PlayerCarrier.TrySnap` asks the aimed collider's parents for a snap pose before free
  placement; `AssemblyStation` implements it (ghost slots, fit messages, install / release, `Carryable.InstalledIn`).
- `DrillDriverTool` (D-035): ray → `AssemblyStation.TryFindOpenPoint` (3 cm) → hold Use → `AssemblyStation.Fasten`
  (domain `TryFasten`, screw head visual, markers updated). Station report text cached per `Changed`. HUD shows
  `AssemblyStation.ReportText` on `Completed` (panel `assembly-report`, 20 s).
- `Carryable` settling (D-031): `RequestSupportCheck` / `RequestSupportCheckAbove`; ray below the center of mass at the
  next FixedUpdate; unsupported → non-kinematic until at rest → kinematic. Called by `PlayerCarrier.TryPickUp` and
  `HandSawTool` after a cut.
- `HandSawTool` (D-030): `BoardAim` + snap to the nearest pencil line (30 mm), evaluates `Crosscut.TryCut` once per
  target, hold `Player/UseTool` for `SawDefinition.SawingSeconds(cross-section)`, stroke animation through
  `PickupItem.HandOffset`; on completion two `Instantiate` copies of the board get `SetPiece`, the original is
  destroyed, the waste goes to `WorkshopWaste.Log`. Orange `CutGuide` line marks the kerf.
- Readout strings are rebuilt only when their inputs change. HUD shows the readout above the crosshair (`tool-readout`).

### Workshop objects (`WoodcraftSimulator.Workshop`)
`LightSwitch`: `IInteractable` toggling a list of `Light`s (first test interactable).

### Editor tooling (`WoodcraftSimulator.Editor`)
`WorkshopSceneBuilder`: menu **WoodcraftSimulator > Build Test Workshop Scene** regenerates the greybox workshop
(40×40 m ground, 8×6 m room with 3 m walls and a door, 0.9 m workbench, timber rack (3 shelves) on the left wall,
9 `TimberBoard`s of all seven species
on the rack and the bench, tape measure + pencil on the bench, workbench lamp + wall switch, player with interactor,
carrier and tool belt, HUD, PauseController, PauseMenu).
Does not modify Build Settings. `WoodSpeciesAssets` creates missing species assets (never overwrites).

## Input actions
Project-wide asset `Assets/InputSystem_Actions.inputactions` (template), extended with:
- `Player/Pause`: `<Keyboard>/escape`, `<Gamepad>/start`.
- `Player/Place`: `<Mouse>/leftButton`, `<Gamepad>/rightTrigger`.
- `Player/Rotate`: `<Keyboard>/r`, `<Gamepad>/rightShoulder`.
- `Player/Tilt`: `<Keyboard>/t`, `<Gamepad>/leftShoulder`.
- `Player/PrecisionLook`: `<Mouse>/rightButton`, `<Gamepad>/leftTrigger`.
- `Player/ToolSlot1..4`: `<Keyboard>/1..4` (no gamepad binding yet). The template's unused `Previous`/`Next`
  are also bound to 1/2; nothing reads them.
